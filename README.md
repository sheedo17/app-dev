# My Favorite Anime Series

## Demon Slayer

**Demon Slayer** is one of my favorite anime series because of its amazing story, interesting characters, and beautiful animation.

### Why I Like It

- The story is exciting.
- The characters have different personalities.
- The animation is amazing.
- It teaches lessons about family, courage, and friendship.

### My Favorite Characters

1. Tanjiro Kamado
2. Nezuko Kamado
3. Zenitsu Agatsuma
4. Inosuke Hashibira

### My Favorite Quote

> Never give up, even when things get difficult.

### Rating

**10/10** – I would recommend this anime to people who enjoy action and adventure stories.
